Foundations

Shape

Arlo's radius scale and the fixed 12px button corner.

  • Buttons, segmented controls, steppers and pop-up buttons: radius-button at every size. The corner is a fixed 12px, not Material's full pill or Apple's capsule, which grow with height. The Button card compares it with the earlier 6px and with a pill.
  • Everything else keeps Arlo's scale: radius-xs checkboxes, radius-mark brand marks and 28px symbol tiles, radius-sm text and search fields, sidebar rows and 44px tiles, radius-md menus, popovers and list groups, radius-lg cards, alerts and sheets, radius-xl large panels.
  • radius-pill is for shapes that are capsules by nature: toggles, badges, progress and slider tracks, page-control dots.
  • Don't mix radius steps inside one group: a 16px card holds 12px (radius-button) buttons and pill badges, not 8px or 6px buttons.

Radius tokens

Arlo's radius scale, unchanged, plus radius-button: one fixed 12px corner for buttons, segmented controls and steppers, instead of Material's full pill or Apple's height-based capsule.

radius-none · 0pxFull-bleed bars: navigation bar, tab bar, toolbars.
radius-xs · 5px16-20px controls: checkbox boxes.
radius-mark · 7pxThe rounded square inside every Arlo brand mark; symbol tiles at 28px.
radius-sm · 8pxText fields, search fields, pop-up menus' items area, sidebar rows, 36-44px symbol tiles.
radius-button · 12pxEvery button, segmented control, stepper and pop-up button, at all sizes (28, 36 and 44px). A fixed 12px corner, not Material's full pill or Apple's capsule: at 28px it is nearly round, at 44px it stays a soft rectangle.
radius-md · 12pxMenus, popovers, inset list groups, banners.
radius-lg · 16pxCards, alerts, sheets (top corners), dialogs.
radius-xl · 24pxLarge panels.
radius-pill · 999pxToggles, badges, tags, progress and slider tracks, page-control dots: shapes that are capsules by nature, not buttons.
