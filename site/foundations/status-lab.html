Status color lab

Success, warning and danger in real Arlo screens. The system uses Apple system colours; compare them with the alternatives that were considered.

Pick a palette: every example below repaints, light and dark side by side.

The colours the system started with, from the Arlo sites. Low-chroma so they sit quietly beside the indigo; green and orange are under 4.5:1 as small text on white, so the system keeps words in ink and puts the colour on symbols.
