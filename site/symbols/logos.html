Brand marks

Arlo's project marks. Never redrawn, never recoloured.

Arlo's project marks, copied as-is from the original Arlo kit: a white glyph in a 28px color-accent (#5856D6) square with a 7px (radius-mark) corner. arlo-mark is the hub ("A"), rust-mark Arlo Rust (">"), lite-mark Arlo Lite (peak with bar), agui-mark AG-UI Rust (waveform); favicon is the hub mark for browser tabs. Use them at 28px or larger, on color-bg or color-bg-secondary. Never recolour or redraw them.
